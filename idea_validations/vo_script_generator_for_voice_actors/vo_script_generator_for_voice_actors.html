The before

The copy looks fine. Then you open your mouth.

Vague briefs

Three bullets and a vibe.

The notice lands thin: product, length, “energetic but not salesy.” No offer hierarchy. No usage. You spend the audition window interpreting the brief instead of acting the read.

Wrong runtime

Word count said :30. Your breath disagreed.

The script was written for the eye. Long sentences. Stacked consonants. A legal line that ate the CTA. You rescue-cut in the booth and hope the client still hears the spot they bought.

Missing direction

Flat copy. Copyright anxiety. Another rewrite.

Practice means scraping someone else’s ad, or prompting a one-size-fits-all tool that ignores genre. The lines look clever and fail out loud. You still do not have rights-cleared sides you can perform on a schedule.

Why the last fix did not stick

A general chatbot can draft more text. It cannot lock a breath window, flag unspeakable phrasing, or tell a bumper from a :60. Timers bolted onto writer-metrics still optimize for the page. The job is a timed performance—spot length, tone, usage, and a read a person can land clean.
